About DKS Construction

Your trusted construction partner in Ratnapura District

Who We Are

D K S Nishantha Construction is a registered contractor based in Lellopitiya, Ratnapura, specializing in civil construction and infrastructure development across the Sabaragamuwa Province.

As an RDA-registered contractor with CIDA C6 grade certification, we bring legitimacy, expertise, and local knowledge to every project we undertake.

Our Approach

We combine modern construction techniques with deep understanding of local terrain and requirements, ensuring projects are delivered with quality and efficiency.

Our strategic location in Lellopitiya positions us perfectly to serve the construction needs of Ratnapura District and surrounding areas.

Our Services

Comprehensive construction solutions tailored to your needs

Civil Construction

Complete civil construction services including buildings, structures, and community facilities with attention to detail and quality.

Infrastructure Projects

Road development, public works, and small to medium-scale infrastructure projects in collaboration with government agencies.

Subcontracting Works

Professional subcontracting services for larger construction firms, leveraging our local expertise and RDA registration.
